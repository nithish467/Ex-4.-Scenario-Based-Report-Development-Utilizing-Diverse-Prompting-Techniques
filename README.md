# Ex-4. Scenario-Based Report Development Utilizing Diverse Prompting Techniques

## Objective

The goal of this experiment is to design and develop an AI-powered chatbot that can handle customer inquiries, provide support, and improve customer experience in a retail environment. Various AI prompting techniques are used to guide the chatbot in understanding customer queries, generating accurate responses, and creating a structured report.

---

## Aim

To develop an AI-powered retail customer-support chatbot using diverse prompt engineering techniques such as Zero-Shot, Few-Shot, Role-Based, Chain-of-Thought, and Structured prompting.

---

## Algorithm

1. Start the experiment.
2. Define the retail customer-support scenario.
3. Identify common customer queries such as product availability, order status, returns, refunds, and complaints.
4. Create prompts using different prompt engineering techniques.
5. Provide the prompts to an AI chatbot.
6. Generate responses for each customer query.
7. Compare the responses obtained using different prompting techniques.
8. Analyze the accuracy, relevance, clarity, and helpfulness of the responses.
9. Prepare a structured report based on the analysis.
10. End the experiment.

---

## Scenario

A retail company wants to introduce an AI-powered chatbot to assist customers with their queries.

The chatbot should be able to:

- Answer product-related questions.
- Check product availability.
- Provide order information.
- Explain return and refund policies.
- Handle customer complaints.
- Recommend suitable products.
- Provide polite and helpful responses.
- Escalate complex issues to human customer support.

---

## Prompt

### 1. Zero-Shot Prompting

**Prompt:**

```text
Analyze the following customer query and provide the response using this structure:

Customer Query:
"I received the wrong product in my order."

Required Format:
1. Problem
2. Apology
3. Solution
4. Required Information
5. Next Step

```

## Output

**Customer Query:**  
"I received the wrong product in my order."

**1. Problem:**  
The customer received a product that is different from the one they ordered.

**2. Apology:**  
We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused.

**3. Solution:**  
We can arrange a replacement with the correct product or provide a refund according to the return policy.

**4. Required Information:**  
Please provide your order number, the product received, and a photo of the item.

**5. Next Step:**  
Contact customer support with the required details to initiate the replacement or refund process.


## Result

Thus, an AI-powered retail customer-support chatbot was successfully designed using diverse prompt engineering techniques. The experiment demonstrated that carefully designed prompts can improve the chatbot's accuracy, relevance, consistency, and customer experience.

## Conclusion

Prompt engineering plays an important role in developing effective AI chatbots. By using techniques such as Zero-Shot, Few-Shot, Role-Based, Chain-of-Thought, and Structured Prompting, the chatbot can provide more accurate and customer-friendly responses. The experiment shows that selecting the appropriate prompting technique based on the scenario can significantly improve AI-generated outputs.
